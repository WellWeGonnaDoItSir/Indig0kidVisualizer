How do I get started?

Upload a song in Audio, choose one of 20 scenes, then open Visuals, Artwork or Text to make it yours. Try the demo to explore before uploading.

Where do my files go?

Audio, images and project files are processed in your browser. The app does not upload them to a server. Optional online fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How does video export work?

Export records the whole track in real time at 2560 × 1440, targeting 60 fps and 385 kb/s audio. Keep this tab visible. Actual performance and available formats depend on your browser and device. Your video contains your artwork and optional text, without website branding.

Can I come back to my project?

Yes. Save project downloads a .indig0kid file containing your audio, artwork, fonts and settings. Import save restores it. Keep your save file: closing the page does not automatically save your work.
